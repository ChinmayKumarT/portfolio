Customer Churn Prediction Service

customerchurn-ckt.netlify.app

Logistic regression benchmarked against a random forest, judged on the metric that matters for churn: recall on the customers who actually leave.

  • Evaluated on ROC-AUC and minority-class recall; threshold chosen for the business, not the default.
  • SHAP attribution traces each prediction back to its input features and turns the output into retention recommendations.
  • Containerised with Docker and deployed publicly.
